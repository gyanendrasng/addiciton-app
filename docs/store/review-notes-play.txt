CRISIS INFORMATION
Crisis lines and a medical disclaimer are in Settings > Support > Get help, and
are deliberately reachable without an account or a subscription.

MEDICAL POSITIONING
Curb is a self-tracking tool for people reducing or stopping a habit. It makes
no treatment claims, is not a medical device, and does not diagnose or treat any
condition. The disclaimer in Get help says so, and advises anyone physically
dependent on alcohol or benzodiazepines to speak to a doctor before stopping.
