Most quitting apps count days. Qwyt is built for the moment the count is about
to break.

Porn, alcohol, smoking, vaping, weed, social media, gambling — or something
you'd rather not name. Qwyt tracks whatever you're stopping, and gives you
something to do at 2am when willpower has already run out.

WHEN AN URGE HITS
Open the urge toolkit and work through it. Box breathing. A two-minute delay,
because that's roughly how long a craving peaks. Your own written reasons, put
back in front of you. A game to occupy your hands until it passes. Qwyt knows
the hour you said your urges are worst, and says so.

A STREAK FOR EVERY HABIT
Quitting two things at once shouldn't mean one slip erases both. Every habit
you track keeps its own count.

SLIPS WITHOUT SHAME
Logging a slip takes three taps, uses no red, and calls it a lapse. Your total
clean days never reset — they record everything you've done, not a score you
can lose. And you have 24 hours to undo it.

WHAT THE DAYS ARE WORTH
The money you haven't spent and the hours you've got back, from your own
figures. What tends to happen in the body and mind after stopping, drawn from
public health guidance, with your progress marked against it.

THE DAILY LOOP
A morning pledge. An evening check-in for mood and difficulty. Milestones at 1,
3, 7, 14, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 and 365 days. A calendar and mood trend once
you've built up history.

BUILT FOR 2AM
Dark and light themes. Reminders you control. No feed, no followers, nothing to
perform. Crisis lines and support are reachable without an account.
