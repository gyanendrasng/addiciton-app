CRISIS INFORMATION
Crisis lines and a medical disclaimer are in Settings > Support > Get help, and
are deliberately reachable without an account or a subscription.

MEDICAL POSITIONING
Nocrave is a self-tracking tool. It makes no treatment claims and the disclaimer in
Get help says so, including advising anyone physically dependent on alcohol or
benzodiazepines to speak to a doctor before stopping.

SCREEN TIME (SHIELD) — iPhone only
The Shield tab lets the user pick apps and websites in Apple's own picker and
keep them out of reach always, during their trigger hours, or for 15–60 minutes
during an urge. This is self-management under the Family Controls entitlement, individual
authorization only: no child accounts, no parental control, and no usage data is
read or transmitted. The shield screen shows one of the user's own reasons and
an "Open Nocrave" button. To test: Shield tab > Allow Screen Time (device passcode
required) > Choose apps and sites > pick any app > Only when I ask > Shield 15 min,
then open that app.
